How it works

Three steps.

CONNECT

Link your key

Type /connect and tap Connect Kalshi. A form opens in Telegram, takes you to Kalshi to make a key, and fills itself in from what you copy. The key is checked with Kalshi and stored encrypted, and never shows in the chat.

ARM

Set the order

/limit BTC yes 40 5 trades now. Add next and it fires at the next opening bell.

EXIT

Get out fast

/close BTC sells it all at the best bid now, or /sell at your price. Set your own caps if you want them; KalBot imposes none.

Combos

Pick winners, spreads and totals across games, and KalBot creates the Kalshi combo market and places your limit order on it.

/combo 1:GB L4 L9 price 15 qty 2

Every rung is checked against your per-order cap, and a bracket that would push your total exposure past your cap is refused before anything is sent. Combo markets often have no resting sellers, so a combo order rests until someone takes it.

Your keys, locked down

Built to hold
trading keys.

  • Encrypted at rest. Keys are stored only in encrypted form, with the decryption key kept in a root-only file on the server.
  • Deleted from the chat. The messages carrying your key are removed the moment KalBot receives them.
  • Checked before saving. A key Kalshi rejects is never stored.
  • Yours to revoke. Use a key made only for KalBot. Revoke it in Kalshi any time, and /disconnect deletes it here.
  • Your commands only. KalBot never trades on its own; it places the orders you set, inside your caps.
